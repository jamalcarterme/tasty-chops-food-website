Our Story

Real Food, Real Flavour, Real Love

Located at House 19, 206 Road, A Close, Festac Town, Lagos, Tasty Chops Foods & Events has become the neighbourhood's most-loved destination for authentic Nigerian chops and event catering.

From crispy puff-puff to perfectly seasoned suya, from intimate birthday spreads to full wedding buffets — every bite we serve is made with fresh ingredients, bold spices and genuine passion. We handle everything so you enjoy the moment.
